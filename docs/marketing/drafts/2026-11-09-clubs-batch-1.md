## 2. Canadian Snowbird Association, CSANews (English)

Why: the national snowbird association (founded 1992, about 110,000 members
in 2017) publishes CSANews for members wintering in the US.
Contact: Evan Rachkovsky, Director of Research and Communications,
Evan.rachkovsky@snowbirds.org

Subject: `A free Canadian app for planning golf and pickleball down south`

```
Hi Evan,

I built BlockCast in Québec: a free, bilingual app that scores every block of the week green, yellow or red for an outdoor sport (golf, pickleball, tennis, cycling, fishing and more) from live forecasts. Snowbirds can save their home town and their winter spot side by side and switch with one tap, and it works in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

It needs no account and collects no data, and the radar comes from Environment Canada's open data. If it fits a CSANews tools or tips item, I'd be glad to send screenshots or anything else you need.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id6796484224
Web: https://blockcast.ca/?ref=csanews

Sandy Scullion
sandy.p.scullion@gmail.com
```
