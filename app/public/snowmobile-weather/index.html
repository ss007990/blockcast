What BlockCast checks for snowmobiling

Every block gets a risk score from 0 to 100: green under 25, yellow under 55, red above. The worst factor drives the score and the others add to it. These are the default weights; you can change any of them in the app under Adjust.

How it works

  1. Pick snowmobiling and your spot. Use your location or search any place in the world, and save up to three.

Snowmobiling weather questions

How much snow do you need to ride?

BlockCast treats ground with less than about 15 cm (6 in) of snow as bare, and snow base carries the full 10 out of 10 weight for snowmobiling. Trails also need to be open: check your club or federation trail report before you load the trailer.

Why does a warm day score badly?

Above freezing the trail softens, ruts up and gets slushy, so heat weighs 7 out of 10 for sleds. The comfortable band is -25 to 4 °C (-13 to 39 °F) of feels-like temperature.

Does BlockCast show trail conditions?

No, it scores the weather at the point you choose. Pair it with your local trail map for grooming and closures.

Where the data comes from

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. Rain is the wettest of three models (the local best match, ECMWF and Canada’s GEM), so a green block means they all agree. Radar is the North American composite from Environment and Climate Change Canada; official alerts come from Environment Canada and the US National Weather Service.
